RELIO Build 30

Build 30 stabilizes trusted Nearby reconnect and connection-session lifecycle.

- Aligns advertising and outgoing request fingerprint-hint metadata.
- Safely verifies trusted identity after a hintless incoming transport, without
  granting private application access before identity proof.
- Keeps first-contact approval and warns before any explicitly approved key
  change; duplicate logical-peer prompts are suppressed.
- Stops discovery/advertising during app lifecycle reconciliation without
  disconnecting established peers.
- Adds caller, reason, active connection/handshake, lifecycle, and route context
  to session-stop diagnostics.

Physical two-device retest remains owner-run and is not claimed by this build
process.
